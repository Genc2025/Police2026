USA CORE · PRACTICE EXAMQUESTION 1 OF 25

A patrol supervisor arrives at a scene where an officer has detained a suspect based on specific and articulable facts suggesting criminal activity. Which constitutional standard most directly governs the initial detention?

Correct answer: B.
A brief investigatory detention is governed by the reasonable-suspicion standard under the Fourth Amendment.
