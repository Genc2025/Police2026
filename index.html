A patrol supervisor arrives at a scene where an officer has detained a suspect based on specific and articulable facts suggesting criminal activity. Which constitutional standard governs the initial detention?

Correct answer: B.
A brief investigatory detention is governed by reasonable suspicion under the Fourth Amendment.
